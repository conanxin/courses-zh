## Key Points / 本章要点

- **Three-part informing skeleton**: Promise → Inspire → Teach How to Think.
  信息型演讲三段式骨架：承诺 → 启发 → 教思维方式。
- **Three inspiration triggers** for three audience types: freshmen (someone says "you can do it"); senior faculty (someone reframes a problem); everyone (passion).
  三类听众需要三种不同的启发按钮：新生（被认可）、资深教职（看到新视角）、所有人（感受到激情）。
- **Storytelling animals**: thinking itself = grasping, asking about, analyzing, combining, and evaluating stories.
  人类是"讲故事的动物"：思考的本质就是把握故事、提问故事、分析故事、组合故事、评估故事。
- **"Incredible" spec-tacular**: a computation that drops from "longer than the solar system" to "a few seconds" is the kind of wow that justifies spending 50 minutes on the topic.
  "把超过太阳系寿命的运算压成几秒钟"就是值得花 50 分钟去讲的那种"惊叹感"。

## Practicable Methods / 可实践方法

1. **50-second opener rule**: in the first 50 seconds, deliver the empowerment promise, then immediately a "wow" beat.
   课堂开场 50 秒法则：用一句话说清承诺，紧接着抛出一个"哇"的反差点。
2. **Slow → fast contrast demo**: start with a deliberately slow program, then show the optimized version, leveraging "lifetime of the solar system" as the unit of contrast.
   "慢 → 快"对比演示：先用故意跑得很慢的程序，再切换到优化版，把"太阳系寿命"作为对比单位。
3. **Self-passion check**: if you can't get excited about your topic, fix the framing or switch topics.
   激情自检法：如果你的研究主题你自己都无法兴奋，那它就不值得讲；要么换讲法，要么换题目。
4. **Five-line story audit** for each domain: must-know stories / must-ask questions / analysis mechanisms / combination methods / evaluation criteria.
   故事能力盘点：把自己研究领域的知识按"必知故事 / 必问问题 / 分析机制 / 组合方法 / 评估方法"五条线整理一遍。
5. **Rephrase "teach people how to think"** into concrete deliverables (stories, questions, mechanisms, combinations, evaluations).
   把"教人思考"翻译成可交付物：故事、问题、机制、组合、评估。
