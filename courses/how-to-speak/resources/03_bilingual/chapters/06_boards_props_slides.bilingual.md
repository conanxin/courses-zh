## Segment 1 / 第一段

**English:**

Next thing I want to talk about is a subject of boards and props and slides.

Well, these are the tools of the trade. I believe that this is the right tool for speaking when your purpose is informing. The slides are good when your purpose is exposing, but this is what I use when I'm informing, teaching, lecturing, and there's several reasons why I use it. For one thing, when you use the board, you have a graphic quality.

It's the case that, when you have a board, then you can easily exploit the fact that you can use graphics in your presentation. So that's the graphic quality that I like, and the next thing I like is the speed property.

The speed with which you write on the blackboard is approximately the speed at which people can absorb ideas. If you go flipping through a bunch of slides, nobody can go that fast.

Finally, one great property of a board is that it can be a target.

## Segment 5 / 第五段

**English:**

Here's an example of the use of a prop, also due to Seymour Papert. He was talking about how it's important to look at the problem in the right way, and here's an example that not only teaches that, but makes it possible for you to embarrass your friends in mechanical engineering.

So here's what you do. Take a bicycle wheel, and you start it spinning. And then you put some torque on the axle, or equivalently, you blow on the edge. And the issue is, does it go that way, or does it go that way?

Now, the mechanical engineers will immediately say, oh, yes, I see—right hand screw wheel. And they'll put their fingers in this position, but forget exactly how to align their fingers with the various aspects of the problem. And so it's usually the case that they get it right with about a 50% probability. So their very fancy education gets them up to the point where they're equivalent to flipping a coin.

But it doesn't have to be that way because you can think about the problem a little differently. So here's what you do. You take some duct tape, and you put it around the part of the wheel like that. And now, you start to think about, not the whole wheel, but just a little piece that's underneath the duct tape. So here, that piece comes rolling over the top, and at this point, you blow on it with a puff of air. Forgetting about the rest of the wheel, what happens to that little piece that's under the duct tape? It must want to go that way because you banged on it like that. It's already going down like that. And what about the next piece? Same thing. Next piece? Same thing. So the only thing that can happen is that the wheel goes over like that. And so now, you'll never wonder again because you're thinking about the problem in the right way, and it's demonstrated by the use of a prop.

You can try this after we're done.

**中文译文:**

下面是另一个使用道具的例子，也来自西摩·派珀特。他正在谈论"用正确的方式看待问题"有多重要，下面这个例子不仅能说明这一点，还能让你有机会让那些学机械工程的朋友尴尬一番。

事情是这样做的：取一个自行车轮，让它旋转起来。然后在车轴上施加一个扭矩，或者等价地，在轮缘上吹一口气。问题是，轮子会往这边转，还是往那边转？

现在，机械工程师们会立刻说："啊，我懂了——右手螺旋定则。"他们会把手指摆成这个姿势，但总是不能精确地让手指对齐问题的各个部分。所以通常情况下，他们的正确率大约只有 50%。也就是说，他们那些精致的教育把他们培养到了相当于"抛硬币"的程度。

但事情本不必如此，因为你可以换一个角度思考这个问题。事情是这样的：你取一些胶带，把它贴在车轮的某一段上，像这样。然后，你开始思考的，不是整个轮子，而是胶带下面那一小段。比如这里，那一小段滚到顶部，在这一点上，你向它吹一口气。忘掉车轮的其他部分，那一小段（也就是胶带下面那一段）会怎么样？由于你是这样吹的，它必然要往那个方向去。它本身又正在这样往下降。下一段呢？也是这样。再下一段？也是这样。所以唯一可能发生的事情，就是轮子会像这样倒下去翻过去。这样一来，你再也不会疑惑了，因为你在用正确的方式思考这个问题，而这一点由道具的使用得到了证明。

## Key Points / 本章要点

- **Boards are best for "informing" talks** because of graphic quality, matching absorption speed, and giving hands a target.
  黑板最适合"告知型"演讲，因为它具有图像化质感、书写速度与听众吸收速度匹配、还能给手提供目标。
- **Props unlock "empathetic mirroring"**: audience mirror neurons fire when they see something physical happen.
  道具可以释放"共情镜像"机制：听众看到真实物理事件时，大脑的镜像神经元会被激活。
- **Slide "crimes" include**: too many words, tiny fonts, redundant titles, logos, bullets, laser pointers that break eye contact, speaker placed too far from screen.
  幻灯片的"罪行"包括：字太多、字号过小、冗余的标题、logo、项目符号、破坏眼神交流的激光笔，以及演讲者离屏幕过远。
- **A hapax legomenon slide** is unintelligible on purpose — you may use one per talk, paper, or book.
  "一次性词（hapax legomenon）"幻灯片故意让人看不懂——每场演讲、每篇论文、每本书最多用一张。
- **Playwrights are the masters of props**: e.g., Ibsen's stove + manuscript in Hedda Gabler.
  剧作家是道具大师：易卜生在《海达·加布勒》中对炉火与手稿的运用即是典范。

## Practicable Methods / 可实践方法

1. **Choose medium by purpose** — boards for teaching/informing; slides for exposing/showing.
   根据演讲目的选择媒介：教学/告知用黑板；展示/呈现用幻灯片。
2. **Keep a 40-50 pt sample slide** as your legibility ruler.
   准备一张 40–50 号最小字号样本幻灯片，作为视觉审稿标尺。
3. **Print-and-spread review** to spot the "too heavy" crime.
   打印审稿法：把整套幻灯片平铺桌面，找出"过重"的演讲。
4. **Five-step slide simplification**: drop background → drop words → drop logo → drop title → drop bullets.
   五步幻灯片简化法：① 删背景 ② 删字 ③ 删 logo ④ 删标题 ⑤ 删项目符号。
5. **Replace laser pointers with on-slide arrows + verbal cues**.
   用图上小箭头配合口头提示，替代激光笔。
6. **Prop design** — place the prop on stage early; let it accumulate meaning; let it climax.
   道具设计——让道具早早出现在舞台上，慢慢积累意义，最后再戏剧性"高潮"。
